About the System

The system brings sales and current stock information together. It shows demand trends, flags products at risk of running out and gives reorder suggestions for the manager to review.

User Roles

  • Wholesaler / Manager: dashboard, demand analysis, alerts, reorder suggestions and reports.
  • Sales staff: record sales.
  • Stock staff: add products and record stock received.
