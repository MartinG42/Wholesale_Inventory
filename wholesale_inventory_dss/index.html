Demand Analysis

Units sold in each 30-day period, worked out from the sales you record. The forecast is the moving average of the three periods. The trend compares the last 30 days with the two periods before it: more than 10% higher is rising, more than 10% lower is falling.

Reorder Suggestions

A suggestion is created when a product's stock falls to its reorder level. Suggested quantity = forecast for the next 30 days + reorder level − available quantity. Change the quantity if needed, then accept or reject it. The final purchasing decision is yours.
